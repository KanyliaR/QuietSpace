Decibel Threshold Sensitivity

Set the relative noise level boundaries for in-room microphones. When room volume crosses these thresholds, the display transitions between Quiet, Warning, and Too Loud.

Level 10
Sound level required to trigger the Yellow warning state on the in-room display. Default: 10.
Level 17
Sound level required to trigger the Red "Too Loud" countdown and create an incident. Must be strictly higher than Yellow. Default: 17.
